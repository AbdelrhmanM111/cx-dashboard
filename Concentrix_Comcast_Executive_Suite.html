About This Project

Built with curiosity,
powered by AI.

Honestly — I simply enjoyed doing this. I had a vision, picked up AI as a collaborative partner, and together we turned 500 call records into a full operational intelligence suite. What surprised me most wasn't the technology — it was how much thinking still sits entirely with the human in the room.

Throughout this project I applied my analytical thinking to uncover real behavioral patterns across a BPO team — identifying who was rushing CPNI verification, who was struggling with diagnostic tools, and who was quietly converting the most Comcast Mobile lines. It refreshed my understanding of how large-scale operations actually work inside major companies like Comcast and Concentrix.

I learnt deeply about modern coaching techniques — how you distinguish a knowledge gap from a behavioral pattern, and what that distinction means for how you lead a team. I'm genuinely excited to keep exploring this space and grow further in it.

And honestly — I'm not worried about whether every number here hits exactly right. This was built as an optimistic scenario, a learning simulation designed to reflect how these operations work in reality. The data is a vehicle, not the destination. What matters is the thinking behind it — and I'm confident that the insights, the frameworks, and the platform we built here can be applied and adapted to any real operation, any team, any company.

Every project like this is a chance to learn more tricks about how operations run — and I'm genuinely looking forward to the next one. This is a beginning, not a finished product.

AI did a tremendous amount of the heavy lifting — building the charts, the logic, the filters. But the insights? Those came from asking the right questions. That part is still us. This project is my way of sharing that idea.

NPS → CX-to-Sales Correlation

High NPS resolution builds trust, directly driving Comcast Mobile line attachment rates.

Gate Zero Security Policy

Non-negotiable compliance gate. Failure = QA 0% + auto-enrollment in compliance module.

Tool Pacing vs. Vanity AHT

Rushing calls <4 min causes misdiagnosis. Thorough diagnostics beat vanity speed metrics.

Promoter Trust Conversion Model

Technical resolution first — then soft-ask mobile pitch. Trust converts, pushes don't.

🔍 Key Data Catches Discovered via Dataset Analytics

Operational Diagnostics Log
1. Vanity AHT vs. CPNI Security Breach

Catch: Calls < 4m AHT (David Lawson & Emily Turner) had a 34.5% CPNI breach rate (29 fails each). Advisors rushed calls and skipped legal PIN authentication to meet vanity handle time targets.

2. Voice LOB Diagnostic & Tool Avoidance

Catch: Voice Inquiry generated -33 NPS and 0% AICR on modem/router calls for Tier 3 advisors in Wk1. Combination of tool avoidance and complex voice troubleshooting knowledge gap.

3. Superficial Reboots Driving 53% Repeat Calls

Catch: Intermittent Connection (46.9% AICR) and Cable Box Rebooting (48.4% AICR) drove over half of all 7-day repeat contacts due to advisors issuing quick box restarts without full signal diagnostics.

4. Navigation Latency Bottleneck (Alex Rivera)

Catch: Alex Rivera (Tier 4) averaged 30.4 min AHT due to tool navigation delays, despite maintaining 100% CPNI pass rate. 10 hrs of shadowing with Sarah Mitchell dropped Alex's AHT by 14 mins by Wk3.

5. Post-Resolution Sales Conversion Timing

Catch: 84% of Mobile Line Sales were generated from NPS 9-10 Promoters post-resolution. Soft-ask sales pitches on unresolved calls had 0% conversion, proving CX resolution must precede upsells.
